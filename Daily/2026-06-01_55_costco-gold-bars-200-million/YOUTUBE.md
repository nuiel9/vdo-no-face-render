# YOUTUBE: Why Is Costco Selling $200 Million a Month in Gold Bars?
Row #55 | Costco gold-bar arbitrage 2024 | UTM: business-pm-costco-gold

## Description

In April 2024, a finance YouTuber bought a one-ounce gold bar at Costco for $2,359 and sold it the next year for $2,955 in cash. He filmed the transaction, posted it to TikTok, and the video has been watched 6.5 million times. The actual story behind it: Costco Wholesale Corporation, quietly and without any press release, became one of the largest retail gold dealers in the United States starting in June 2023. By April 2024 Wells Fargo estimated the company was selling between $100 million and $200 million a month in 1-ounce PAMP Suisse and Rand Refinery gold bars at roughly 2 percent above spot. The margin barely covers shipping. This is not really a gold business. This is the same brand-trust mechanic as the $1.50 hot dog, applied to bullion.

## Pinned Comment Draft

Source check. The $100-200M/month gold-bar revenue estimate is from Wells Fargo equity analyst Edward Kelly's April 9, 2024 research note, covered by CNBC, Fortune, CBS News, and ABC News. The $100 million Q1 FY2024 figure was disclosed directly by Costco then-CFO Richard Galanti on the December 2023 earnings call. Costco does NOT break out gold-bar revenue in its 10-Q filings, so the monthly figure is an analyst estimate, not a Costco-disclosed number. The Humphrey Yang transaction ($2,359 purchase, $2,955.42 sale at Witter Coin) is his own documented TikTok video — note that his profit was over an approximately 12-month hold (purchased early 2023, sold April 2024), not a same-day flip. The 5-bar purchase limit is attested by multiple trade-press sources but I have not found a Costco-issued statement on it.
