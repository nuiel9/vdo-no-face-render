## Thesis

Costco Wholesale Corporation quietly launched 1-ounce gold bars on costco.com in June 2023 with no press release. By April 2024, Wells Fargo estimated the program was generating $100-200 million per month in revenue at approximately 2% above the spot price of gold. That 2% margin barely covers shipping and warehousing overhead — meaning Costco is **not really running a gold business.** The gold-bar program is a brand-trust extension play, structurally identical to the $1.50 hot dog: a deliberate loss-leader designed to reinforce the membership renewal that produces the majority of Costco's operating profit. The strategic decision is to take on the mechanical-control layer of a new product category, accept the thin margin, and use the control to extend the Costco brand promise into bullion.

## 5 Contrarian Angles

1. **Costco's gold business is a deliberate loss-leader, not a revenue category.** Despite generating up to $200M/month in revenue, the ~2% margin means the program nets a few million dollars/month at most. In a quarter where Costco reports $1B+ in net income, gold bars are rounding error. The strategic value is membership-renewal-and-acquisition, not gold-sales profit.

2. **The 2% margin is the entire story.** Every other US gold dealer charges 4-10% over spot. Costco's 2% markup is so aggressive that customers with stacked cash-back can buy at or below the live spot price — economically impossible in traditional retail bullion. That's the brand-promise mechanic at work.

3. **The disclosure happened by accident.** Galanti's December 2023 earnings-call mention of "$100M in gold bars" was buried in a list of product categories. There was no investor-day announcement. The public learned about the scale of the program only because the Reddit warehouse-shopping subforum noticed it first.

4. **PAMP Suisse + Rand Refinery is the same supplier base as institutional bullion buyers.** Costco isn't selling tourist-grade gold — it's selling LBMA Good Delivery-equivalent material from the same refiners that supply Goldman Sachs, central banks, and bullion exchanges. The 1oz bars are functionally interchangeable with what trades on the COMEX.

5. **The 90% membership renewal rate IS the strategy.** Costco's structural advantage over Walmart/Kroger/Target is that membership fees produce the majority of operating income. Every deliberately-thin-margin product category — hot dog, rotisserie chicken, gas, now gold — exists to reinforce the renewal decision. The gold bars are just the most recent iteration of a 40-year-old playbook.

## 6 Lesser-Known Facts

1. **PAMP Suisse is based in Castel San Pietro, Switzerland**, founded as a separate brand in 1977, accredited by the London Bullion Market Association as a Good Delivery refiner — the global gold-trading standards body. Source: PAMP corporate site / LBMA Good Delivery List.

2. **Rand Refinery has historically processed roughly one-third of all the gold ever mined**, founded in 1920, located in Germiston, South Africa. It is the largest single gold-refining facility in the world by cumulative throughput. Source: Rand Refinery corporate history.

3. **Costco's gold bars sell at approximately 2% above the live spot price** — the lowest retail bullion markup in the United States. Comparable dealers charge 4-10% (APMEX, JM Bullion, Provident Metals). Source: Wells Fargo Kelly note, bullion-dealer comparisons.

4. **Costco's fiscal 2024 membership fees totaled approximately $4.8 billion**, accounting for the majority of the company's operating income. The product-margin contribution is structurally lower. Source: Costco 10-K FY2024.

5. **CFO Richard Galanti retired March 15, 2024 after 39 years at Costco.** He was the public face of every Costco earnings call from 1985 onwards, and his casual disclosure of the $100M gold-bar figure on the December 2023 call (his second-to-last as CFO) was the moment Wall Street understood the program's scale. Gary Millerchip took over. Source: Costco corporate announcement.

6. **The Humphrey Yang TikTok arbitrage video has 6.5 million views as of mid-2025**, posted from @humphreytalks. The transaction was filmed at Witter Coin, a San Francisco-based bullion dealer that has been in business since 1962. Yang's profit was $595.43 over an approximately 12-month hold period (he bought the bar in spring 2023, sold April 2024).
