# YOUTUBE: The Blackberry meeting where they ignored the touchscreen
Row #7 | BlackBerry rise and fall | UTM: business-pm-07

## Description

In January 2007, Mike Lazaridis watched the iPhone keynote in Waterloo, walked to his co-CEO Jim Balsillie's office, and showed him what Steve Jobs had just announced. Balsillie's reply, according to the 2015 book "Losing the Signal": "It's OK. We'll be fine." Eighteen months later RIM hit its all-time peak market cap of roughly seventy-five billion dollars. Six years after that, BlackBerry's global smartphone market share was under one percent and the company posted a five point nine billion dollar annual loss. This is the story of the meeting, the keyboard argument, the panic Storm launch, the four-year enterprise pocket that delayed the reckoning, and what Lazaridis quietly understood that the company never acted on.
