Custom designer brief (NOT AIVDO poster mode default)

Concept: a single transparent hex-barreled Bic Cristal ballpoint pen, photographed top-down on a textured matte off-white surface, with the absurd number "120,000,000,000" overlaid in bold black sans-serif type next to the pen. The pen is centered slightly off the rule-of-thirds left, the number floats to the right of it. A subtle film-grain overlay and a faint warm vignette give the image an editorial-magazine feel rather than a generic stock-photo look.

Typography: number set in a bold geometric sans-serif (Akzidenz Grotesk Bold or Inter Display ExtraBold), kerned tight, all numerals. Below the number, a smaller secondary line in the same family: "ONE PEN. ONE BET." in tracked uppercase.

Color palette: matte off-white background #F4F1EA, brass pen tip #B8895A, transparent blue ink #2A3A75, type in #1A1A1A. Avoid pure white. Avoid pure black.

Mobile legibility check: the number must read at 320px width. Number target height: at least 22% of total thumbnail height.

Avoid: AIVDO default poster mode template, generic Bic-brand logo treatments, stock photo aesthetic, person holding pen, gradient backgrounds, text drop shadows.

The curiosity comes from the absurdity of the number against the smallness of the object. Do not overdesign it.
