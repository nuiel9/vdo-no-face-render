Row #33 | docusign-pandemic-overshoot | UTM: business-pm-docusign

## Description

In September 2021, DocuSign stock touched $310. Eighteen months later it traded at $39. The company had cycled through two CEOs, three rounds of layoffs, an activist investor, and a collapsed $16B take-private bid. This is not a video about e-signature. It is a video about the gap between subscription growth and subscription health: how net retention above 100% can mask gross retention deterioration, and why the financial calendar is the slowest measurement system in any subscription business. With the same retention math that broke Peloton's churn model in a consumer context, applied here to B2B SaaS at scale.

If that is the kind of analysis you want more of, subscribe to Disclosed. We ship three case studies a week.
