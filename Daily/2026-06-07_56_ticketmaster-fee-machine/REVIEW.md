## Source ledger (every spoken specific)

1. **Merger cleared Jan 25 2010 + 10-yr consent decree** — DOJ permitted Live Nation + Ticketmaster merger, required a 10-year consent decree against anticompetitive conduct. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_v._Live_Nation_Entertainment
2. **5 consent-decree violations; 2020 extension to 2025 + $3M** — DOJ found Live Nation violated the decree "repeatedly and over the course of several years" (five instances, incl. pressure on venues using rival ticketers); 2019/2020 the decree was amended + extended to 2025 and LN paid $3M enforcement costs. https://www.billboard.com/pro/live-nation-doj-consent-decree-violations/ + https://www.ticketnews.com/2020/01/live-nation-consent-decree-doj-slap-on-wrist/
3. **GAO 2018: primary fees avg 27%, up to 58%** — GAO-18-347: in a nongeneralizable sample, primary-market total fees averaged 27% of ticket price (range 13–58%); secondary avg 31%. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-18-347
4. **Eras Tour Nov 15 2022: 3.5M Verified Fan, site crashed in an hour, 2.4M sold (record)** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift%E2%80%93Ticketmaster_controversy
5. **General public sale canceled (~Nov 17 2022), "extraordinarily high demand"/"insufficient remaining ticket inventory"** — same source as #4.
6. **Senate Judiciary hearing Jan 24 2023** — Live Nation President Joe Berchtold testified. (Narration says "a company executive" — no name spoken.) Source #4 + NPR.
7. **DOJ + ~40 states + DC sued May 23 2024, sought breakup** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_v._Live_Nation_Entertainment + https://www.npr.org/2026/03/03/nx-s1-5728962/live-nation-ticketmaster-trial-explainer
8. **March 2026: DOJ SETTLEMENT — ~$200M to states + structural changes, AVOIDED breakup; some states declined** — https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/ticketmaster-live-nation-settles-antitrust-case-rcna262392 + https://www.npr.org/2026/03/09/nx-s1-5742433/live-nation-ticketmaster-doj-antitrust-case
9. **April 15 2026: STATES' jury verdict — liable on every antitrust count (monopolized primary ticketing + illegal bundling, overcharged fans); remedy TBD by Judge Arun Subramanian** — https://www.npr.org/2026/04/15/nx-s1-5786715/live-nation-ticketmaster-antitrust-verdict-monopoly + https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/15/politics/ticketmaster-live-nation-monopoly-verdict
