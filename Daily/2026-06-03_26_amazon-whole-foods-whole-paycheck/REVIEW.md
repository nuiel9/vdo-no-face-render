## Thesis

In 2017 Amazon, the most ruthless price-cutter in retail history, paid $13.7B for the most expensive grocer in America and promised to end "Whole Paycheck." It cut commodity prices hard on day one. But the cuts were narrow (the easily-compared commodities), the basket stayed ~13% above conventional rivals, and by early 2019 prices were rising again as supplier contracts expired. The structural truth: Whole Foods was never expensive because of its bananas — it was expensive because of premium sourcing, quality standards, prime real estate, and labor. That cost is the product. You cannot data-optimize it away. "Whole Paycheck" was never a bug to fix; it was the business.

## Editorial caveats (hand-typed, Human Fingerprint #2)

- **"More than four hundred and fifty stores."** At the Aug 2017 close Whole Foods operated ~460-470 US stores; I used the conservative "more than 450" rather than a precise count. Safe.
- **The "as much as 43%" figure.** Bloomberg's URL slug says "50%" but the article headline and body cite "as much as 43%." 43% is the figure carried by all corroborating coverage. Narration uses 43%.
- **Feb-2019 numbers were conflated across sources and deliberately softened.** Fortune gives "550 items raised Dec 2018 + ~50 more, 700 contracts expired, avg 66 cents." CBS/TODAY frame it as "raise-or-discontinue 700 products, ~half dropped, ~100 renegotiated, ~50 raised." These are not the same breakdown. Narration uses only the safely-supported intersection: "roughly 700 supplier contracts expiring, hundreds of items up, average 66 cents." I did NOT assert the precise discontinued/renegotiated split.
- **Market-share figures are rounded.** Numerator Q1 2022 = 1.3% WF / 21.3% Walmart; CNBC's 2022 phrasing = "just over 1%" / "Walmart 19%." Narration says "about one percent" / "roughly twenty" to stay inside both.
- **No named-person visuals.** Per faceless rule + memory, zero Bezos/Mackey likenesses or silhouettes anywhere in the scene prompts. Scene 13 (Mackey retirement) uses an empty-office environment only.
