# YOUTUBE: Why McDonald's Ice Cream Is Always Broken (It Was Never About the Machine)
Row #30 | McDonald's ice cream machine saga | UTM: business-pm-30

In October 2020, a software engineer built McBroken.com in a single afternoon. It showed one in six U.S. McDonald's locations had a broken soft-serve machine. He didn't hack anything. He just asked McDonald's public app the same question you'd ask at the counter. What the data exposed wasn't a broken machine. It was a franchise contract that locked 40,000 locations into a single approved vendor with no exit clause. This is the story of Kytch, a $300 device, a trade secret lawsuit, and a federal report that put the most profitable franchise system in the world on the record.
