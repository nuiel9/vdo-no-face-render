## Source ledger (every spoken specific)

1. **2002 IRS Free File deal; IRS agreed not to build its own free tool** — Free File Alliance agreement; gov't refrained from competing in exchange for industry free-filing offers. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRS_Free_File
2. **ProPublica 2019: Intuit added code hiding the genuinely-free Free File page from search engines** — https://www.propublica.org/article/turbotax-deliberately-hides-its-free-file-page-from-search-engines
3. **~two-thirds of taxpayers couldn't use the free product (FTC)** — FTC found the "free" ads deceptive; ~2/3 ineligible. https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/01/23/the-ftc-bars-turbotax-maker-intuit-advertising-deceptive-free-services
4. **May 2022: $141M settlement, all 50 states + DC, ~4.4M customers steered into paying** — led by NY AG. https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/01/23/the-ftc-bars-turbotax-maker-intuit-advertising-deceptive-free-services (settlement also widely reported AP/NPR)
5. **Jan 2024: FTC final order barred Intuit from advertising "free" unless free for all or clearly disclosing who doesn't qualify; deceptive ads "for at least six years"** — https://www.gpb.org/news/2024/01/23/the-ftc-bars-turbotax-maker-intuit-advertising-deceptive-free-services + https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/09/nine-takeaways-initial-decision-intuit-turbotax-action
6. **Intuit left the IRS Free File program in 2021 (H&R Block left 2020); Intuit said it left due to program limitations** — https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2022/03/intuit-spends-millions-lobbying-amid-accusations-of-deceptive-turbotax-advertising/
7. **Lobbying: >$3M (2021), record ~$3.7M (2024), much aimed at IRS free-filing** — https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2024/02/turbotax-maker-intuit-spent-millions-in-record-lobbying-blitz-amid-threats-to-tax-prep-industry/
8. **IRS Direct File: 2024 launch, expanded to 25 states / 30M+ eligible** — https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2023/11/irs-free-tax-filing-rollout-faces-hurdles-after-multimillion-dollar-lobbying-campaign/
9. **~296,000 Direct File users last year → 0 this year (program shut down for the 2026 season)** — https://fortune.com/2026/04/15/tax-filing-lobbying-intuit-hr-block-irs-direct-file/
