## Description

In August 2017, MoviePass launched a nine ninety-five a month unlimited movie pass that everyone agreed was structurally unsustainable. The financial press treated the company's eventual collapse as the obvious endpoint of an obvious problem with the unit economics.

The federal cases that arrived five years later told a different story. The Securities and Exchange Commission and the Southern District of New York went after the executives of Helios and Matheson Analytics, MoviePass's parent company, for what they told investors during the capital raises. The case is about disclosure, not pricing.

This is a breakdown of the gap between the customer-facing story and the investor-facing story, and what federal prosecutors allege happened in that gap.
