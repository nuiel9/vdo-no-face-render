# YOUTUBE: The 65-Year Tupperware Party Is Over
Row #40 | Tupperware 1948-2024 collapse | UTM: business-pm-40

## Description

On September 17th, 2024, Tupperware Brands Corporation filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy with eight hundred and eleven million dollars in debt. The company had been in business for seventy-six years. Its product was sold in more than a hundred countries. At its peak it had over a million independent home-party salespeople working a sales model that nobody else in American retail had ever made work at scale. This video traces the strategic decision that built Tupperware: Earl Tupper's 1947 plastic patent, Brownie Wise's home-party invention, Tupper firing her in 1958 and erasing her from company history, the sixty-five-year direct-sales run, the company's refusal to build e-commerce as the internet arrived, and the September 2024 lender-controlled liquidation. The same decision that built Tupperware killed it.
