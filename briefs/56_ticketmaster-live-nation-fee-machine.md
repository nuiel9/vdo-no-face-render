## Framing discipline (LOAD-BEARING)

This is a **BUSINESS POSTMORTEM**, NOT a consumer shopping-complaint. "Why are Ticketmaster fees so high" is the angle news and late-night already own. The spine is: **how the fee machine was structurally built, and why winning in court still won't dismantle it.**

Spine: 2010 merger + consent decree → the fee/venue/promotion flywheel → 2022 Eras Tour exposed it → 2024 DOJ suit → April 2026 monopoly verdict → the structural reason the fees persist anyway (vertical integration: promotion + ticketing + venues under one roof; the fee isn't a bug, it's how the integrated machine monetizes a captive market).

Thesis (mirror of the #26 "the cost IS the product" structural truth): **the fees were never an overcharge to be regulated away — they are the business model of a vertically integrated monopoly, which is exactly why a courtroom win doesn't lower them.**

## Verified facts to anchor (confirm/refresh in the 17-prompt research + editorial gate)

- **2010:** Live Nation + Ticketmaster merged; DOJ imposed a consent decree (10-year, later extended).
- **Nov 2022:** Taylor Swift *Eras Tour* presale collapsed; Ticketmaster site failures; Senate Judiciary hearing Jan 2023.
- **May 23 2024:** DOJ + ~40 states + DC filed an antitrust suit alleging Live Nation monopolized the live-events sector.
- **March 2026:** DOJ reached a SETTLEMENT (~$200M to participating states + structural changes); Live Nation AVOIDED a breakup. NY AG Letitia James + other states declined the settlement and continued.
- **April 15 2026:** a federal jury found Live Nation/Ticketmaster liable on the STATES' antitrust claims — illegal monopolization of primary ticketing + illegal bundling; overcharged consumers. Remedy to be set by Judge Arun Subramanian. (~8 weeks before ship — strongly current.)
